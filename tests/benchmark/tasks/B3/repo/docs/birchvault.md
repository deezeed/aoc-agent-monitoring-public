# Birchvault -- offer summary

Birchvault (Vienna) quoted 21 EUR per TB per month for the volume we asked
about (40 TB), billed monthly. All storage nodes are in Austria and Finland;
the contract has an EU data-residency clause.

Their SLA is 99.95 % monthly uptime, with service credits of 10 % below that
and 25 % below 99.0 %. Restores are free up to 2 TB per month. Onboarding
takes about two weeks.
