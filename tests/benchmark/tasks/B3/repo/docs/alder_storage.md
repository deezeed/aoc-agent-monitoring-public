# Alder Storage -- call notes (sales, 12 Oct)

Alder runs two data centres, Frankfurt and Amsterdam, and contractually keeps
customer data inside the EU. Their sales rep was keen to talk about the new
dashboard. List price for cold storage is 18 EUR per TB per month, with no
egress fees for restores under 1 TB a month.

Uptime: the standard contract guarantees 99.5 % monthly availability; the rep
said "in practice we're far above that" but would not put a higher number in
writing. Support is email only on the standard tier.
